Why DM Hire
8 confirmed gaps in the current DM ATS — and exactly how we solve them
🎯
Directly from Brandon's walkthrough + Emma's scoping session
Every item below was confirmed as a real gap in the current DM Payroll ATS — not assumed. DM Hire was designed to close all of them while keeping the seamless onboarding → payroll handoff that already works.
8
Gaps Closed
📄
Resume Parsing
Gap called out by Brandon as "a big one"
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
Applicants upload resume and manually re-enter every field — name, work history, education, references. Slow, friction-heavy, high drop-off.
DM Hire
Upload resume once — AI auto-fills every field. Applicants review and confirm in 60 seconds. Recruiter sees parsed data instantly in candidate profile.
✦ See it in action → open any Candidate Profile and look for the "Resume auto-parsed" indicator
📊
Applicant Source Tracking
Flagged as important to DM specifically
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
Zero visibility into where applicants come from. LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter — all look the same. No way to know which job boards are worth the spend.
DM Hire
Every applicant is tagged by source at the moment they apply. Reports show conversion rate, time-to-hire, and cost-per-hire by source — down to the job level.
✦ See it in action → Reports & Analytics → Source ROI table
🔗
Per-Job Direct Apply Links
Emma pushed on this directly — LinkedIn posts need a direct link
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
One URL for the entire job list — no per-job links. LinkedIn posts land candidates on a wall of all open jobs. Individual job IDs in the URL are guessable integers (e.g. "/job/12").
DM Hire
Every job gets a clean, obfuscated direct apply URL. One click copies it for LinkedIn, Indeed, email, or your careers page. QR code auto-generated for print.
✦ See it in action → Job Requisitions → click "Share Link" on any open job
✍
True Electronic Signatures
Emma flagged as high-priority — legal enforceability concern
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
Not a real e-signature — just a timestamped name entry. Brandon confirmed this explicitly. Offer letters, W-4s, and handbook acknowledgments are all using this weaker method.
DM Hire
ESIGN/UETA-compliant e-signature widget — reusable across offer letters, I-9s, W-4s, and handbook docs. Full audit trail with IP, timestamp, and signature certificate.
✦ Emma noted this should be a shared widget across MyInfo + ATS — that's exactly how it's built
📅
Outlook Interview Self-Scheduling
Multiple clients and prospects have asked for this
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
No calendar integration. Recruiter emails candidate, candidate replies, recruiter manually creates the invite. Back-and-forth delays the process by 1–3 days on average.
DM Hire
Recruiter shares available slots in one click. Candidate self-selects, confirms, and receives an Outlook/Teams invite automatically. Reschedules handled the same way.
✦ See it in action → Candidate Pipeline → click "📅 Self-Schedule" on any interview-stage card
🚦
True Knockout Questions
Current "knockout" is just waiver acknowledgment — not real disqualifying logic
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
No real disqualifying logic. Any applicant can complete and submit regardless of their answers. Recruiters manually identify unqualified candidates — wastes time reviewing every submission.
DM Hire
Per-job knockout rules set in requisition. Unqualified applicants can still submit, but receive a delayed rejection (1–2 days, not instant) so it doesn't feel abrupt. Qualified applicants are auto-surfaced.
✦ See it in action → New Requisition form → "Knockout Questions" section
🔍
Duplicate Candidate Detection
ClearCompany was named as the benchmark — DM ATS has nothing
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
Candidates matched only by email address. Same person applying with two email addresses creates two unlinked profiles — notes, history, and prior interviews aren't connected.
DM Hire
Multi-signal matching: name + phone + resume fingerprint flags likely duplicates. Recruiters see a merge prompt. Full cross-application history is linked in one unified profile.
✦ See it in action → Candidate Pipeline → look for the duplicate warning badge on Chris Lawson
📢
Automated Job Board Distribution
Brandon confirmed this as a real gap — manual posting today
Gap Today
Current DM ATS
Clients manually post to each job board separately. No single-point distribution. No tracking of which boards are connected to which postings.
DM Hire
Approve a requisition → choose boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor) → one click publishes everywhere simultaneously. All postings managed from one screen.
✦ See it in action → Job Requisitions → Integrations tab → board sync status
What doesn't change
The ATS → Onboarding → DM Payroll handoff stays seamless.
Cassie's requirement: ATS must continue to feed directly into onboarding as an entry point. That's untouched. Candidate accepted → onboarding packet auto-assigned → employee record syncs to DM Payroll → time & attendance, benefits, and 401(k) recordkeeper all flow from that same record.
